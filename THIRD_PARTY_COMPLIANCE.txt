THIRD-PARTY COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

This repository does not relicense third-party software, model weights, datasets, or audio assets.

Review before sharing or deploying:

1. Python package licenses
   - FastAPI
   - Uvicorn
   - Pydantic
   - HTTPX
   - PyTorch
   - Torchaudio
   - Transformers
   - Accelerate
   - IBM Adversarial Robustness Toolbox
   - Librosa
   - SoundFile

2. Hugging Face models and assets
   - confirm each model's license;
   - confirm whether commercial use is allowed;
   - confirm whether redistribution of weights is allowed;
   - confirm gated or access-restricted terms if applicable.

3. Audio samples
   - confirm you have the right to store, test, and share the audio;
   - avoid committing customer or personal data;
   - avoid committing generated reports containing sensitive data.

4. Wrapper code
   - confirm user-supplied wrappers are either original or properly licensed;
   - do not accept copied proprietary wrappers without approval.

5. Release hygiene
   - do not commit downloaded model weights;
   - do not commit virtual environments;
   - do not commit runtime scan artifacts unless explicitly intended;
   - review generated reports before sharing.
